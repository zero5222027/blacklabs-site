What this website collects

When you write to us

If you send us an email, we process your email address, your name if you provide it, and the content of your message, in order to answer you. Our mailboxes are hosted by Zoho Mail in the European Union. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and to meet legal obligations, and then delete it.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers that host this website and our email, and with public authorities when the law requires it.

Your rights

You may ask us for access to your personal data, for its correction or deletion, for restriction of or objection to its processing, and for a copy of it. Write to privacy@blacklabs.works; we answer within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country; in the Republic of Moldova this is the National Center for Personal Data Protection.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date.
